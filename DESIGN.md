# Design System Documentation

This document outlines the core aesthetic and functional principles of our design system, ensuring consistency and coherence across all user interfaces.

## 1. Theme Configuration

### Color Mode
The UI operates in **Dark Mode**, providing a comfortable viewing experience in low-light environments and reducing eye strain.

### Core Colors
The design utilizes a custom color palette, with specific hex values assigned to primary, secondary, tertiary, and neutral roles:
*   **Primary Color:** `#ffaa00` - A vibrant orange, serving as the brand's most distinctive chromatic color. It's suitable for primary buttons, call-to-action elements, and key interactive components.
*   **Secondary Color:** `#977038` - A warm, earthy brown that supports less prominent UI elements, chips, and secondary actions.
*   **Tertiary Color:** `#07cbff` - A bright blue used as an additional accent for highlights, badges, or decorative elements.
*   **Neutral Color:** `#817568` - A soft, warm gray providing a neutral base for backgrounds, surfaces, and non-chromatic elements.

### Typography
The system employs a carefully selected set of typefaces to establish clear hierarchy and enhance readability:
*   **Headline Font:** `Space Grotesk` - A modern, geometric sans-serif, chosen for its strong presence and legibility in headlines.
*   **Body Font:** `Inter` - A highly versatile and readable sans-serif, ideal for long-form text and general body content.
*   **Label Font:** `Inter` - Consistent with the body font, `Inter` is also used for labels to maintain uniformity and clarity across interactive elements.

### Roundedness
UI elements feature a **subtle roundedness (1)**, offering a soft touch without appearing overly rounded. This strikes a balance between modern aesthetics and crispness.

### Spacing
The design system adopts **normal spacing (2)**, providing adequate whitespace within and between UI elements. This ensures a balanced visual density, aiding readability and user interaction without feeling too cramped or overly spacious.

## 2. Component Guidelines (Examples)

### Buttons
Buttons will primarily use the **Primary Color (`#ffaa00`)** for their backgrounds, with legible text. Secondary actions may utilize the **Secondary Color (`#977038`)** or outlined styles. All buttons will adhere to the **subtle roundedness (1)** for their corners and respect the **normal spacing (2)** for internal padding and margins.

### Cards
Cards will use the **Neutral Color (`#817568`)** as their background base, with content structured using **normal spacing (2)**. Their corners will also feature **subtle roundedness (1)**.

### Text Fields
Text fields will maintain a clean appearance with `Inter` as the primary font for input and labels. Borders will be subtle, and corners will match the **subtle roundedness (1)** of other UI elements.
